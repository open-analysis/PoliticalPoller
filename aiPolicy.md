# Artificial Intelligence Policy
Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models = AI for brevity
## Expectations
AI may be used for function/method/class descriptions \
AI may be used for code review \
AI may be used for minor debug \
AI may be used to assist development, however the contributor must:
1. Disclose AI/LLM usage as documented in [File Policy](filePolicy.md)
    1. If used in review/debug/analysis/documentation, please note in the commit message with the same format as in the [File Policy](filePolicy.md)
2. Ensure technical accuracy and commit scope
3. Understand the submitted code. Be able to assist reviewers if questions arise
4. Do not contribute sensitive, private, false, or otherwise confidential information, functions, or variables


### Reasoning
I have gone back and forth on this issue (as noted by the previous commits to this project). \
I am trying to determine a course that I feel is ethical in software engineering as a whole, and \
morally responsible for as a creator and owner of this project. I have landed on this decision \
after reviewing several other open source projects' AI policy's and reasonings behind them. \

This policy may be revisited if there's enough interest in the maintaining parties to do so.
